Light beamed into the world,  
but men and women ran towards the darkness.  

The mountain cast its shadow before the dawn.  
Faces vanished beneath masks of stone.  
Crowds moved as one, yet each believed they were alone.  

There was no beginning.  
There was no end.  
Only the silence that followed.
And the end of the game wasn't far away.


A pawn moves forward, though the path is blind.  
Each step is heavier than the last,  
yet the board does not bend beneath him.  

Some call it duty,  
others call it sacrifice.  
But no one asks if the march was chosen.  

At the edge lies promise,  
or silence.  
A crown, or nothing at all.  


Yet, the pawn is the lowest piece.  
It is born with no value, not even a name.  
It moves forward without choice, without voice.  

Theory calls it the skydiver of death:  
always falling, never flying,  
and when it lands,
no one will shed a tear for him.  

The pawn's fate is simple —  
to walk until it breaks,  
or to reach a line it was never meant to see.


Nothing was revealed.  
Nothing was hidden.  
Only the endless waiting that it brought,
and the dark fantasy that breaks the mind.

And when the last sound fell quiet,  
there was no beginning left to name.
